i hate starting my sentences with capitals.

but i love my punctuation.

i dream, i weave, i make.

but i hate capitals.

Only when they have a purpose do i use them.

for example,

i am not important. who am i to need such grandeur as a larger letter?

what does the start of a sentence mean? why does it need such an abrupt start?

words splashed on a page

are still read, regardless of their stature.

so now i’ve made my Defence. is the Jury out? can i have my Freedom?

I Am a Writer. I do What I PleAse.
