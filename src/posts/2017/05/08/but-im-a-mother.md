There's spit up on the thigh of my pant. More grease in my hair than I'd like to admit. The bags of my eyes have bags.

But that's alright: I'm a mother.

There's laundry two weeks overdue. Salt like sand building up in the front hall that needs sweeping. Hair balls forming in the deep recesses of the bathroom.

But that's alright: I'm a mother.

One ear is ringing from screams about past bedtime. And action? Let's just say it's been more than a month. I can't remember the last time I wasn't holding a tiny body.

But that's alright: I'm a mother.
