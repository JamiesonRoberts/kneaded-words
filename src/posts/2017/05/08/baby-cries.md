Every cry feels like an accusation. An audible dagger to the heart which gushes guilt. It doesn't matter if you are hungry or dehydrated or even chained to the toilet with unmentionables: that sound will punish. It doesn't matter that she does not yet have complexity of emotion: that she cannot carry a grudge. It doesn't matter how many times you tell yourself that she'll turn out just fine all the same. All the logic in the world cannot shield you from that self destructive reaction: your heart bleeds, your chest aches, your eyes water. And you pick her up. Gently hold her; nurse her; sooth her.

But the wound remains.
