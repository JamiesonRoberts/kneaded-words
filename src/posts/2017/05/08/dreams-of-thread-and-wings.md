I used to be a dreamer

I used to find joy in life And never take myself too seriously.

Head in the clouds, I saw everything Little details made me wonder with awe.

But you pulled me down to the ground. Threw a string around my foot and staked it there. Told me I could never leave.

"You are a woman. You are a wife. You are a mother.

You are only these things and no more and no less. You do not stray."

Well I pull at your strings. Tug at your chains. Yank and yank until that stake comes flying out.

I will not be still.

I will not be only what you tell me to be.

I will re find my dreamer self and share her with my daughter so that she may dream too. I will open my eyes wider with each day so that she may see what I saw so long ago.

I will spread my wings and support myself because I can. And under my wing my little one will fly with me.

Because she can.

Because we can.
