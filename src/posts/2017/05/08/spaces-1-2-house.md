_drip._

_drip._

_der Hahn,_              

_drip.drip.drip…_

_…das macht nichts…_           

~

A house.

Standing alone, even though surrounded by other homes.

But they are homes. This is just a house.

Inside is just as bare (as out’) devoid of any- thing

any- life

any- joy.

no children no photos no quilts (and) no grammaphone

just an air, an air of emptiness.

      _so leer…_

There are things, plenty of things.

an empty hearth an empty book.shelf an empty

   life?

There’s

a ghost in the corner

a chair 

and a mourner…

     *Ach,*

     *Veilleicht… Veilleicht…*

A crumpled thing, in a creaky thing, without anything, in an empty house…

_Wenn …nur…_
