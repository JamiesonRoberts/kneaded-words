Actually, this is more like "What I have planned for the next two weeks." Since reaching the completion of eight months pregnant, I'm slowly losing steam in the kitchen (note the sink overflowing with tupperware at the moment). But I'm still trying my darnedest to keep cooking because eating out won't help me feel any more energetic than it will help our budget -or my already expanded waistline for that matter.

I accidentally filled up the first week of this two-week cycle with mostly vegetarian options by accident, though I may still opt to add meat to some of them after all. Here's what I'm hoping my two weeks in the kitchen looks like:

**Today:** Smitten Kitchen's [Buttermilk Roast Chicken](http://smittenkitchen.com/blog/2012/01/buttermilk-roast-chicken/) with vegetable sides **Tomorrow:** Breakfast for dinner, _buttermilk pancakes_, bacon, maybe some fruit **Sunday:** leftovers (we're going to be at a prenatal class all weekend so easy meals were a must. Pancakes actually freeze pretty well as a side note.) **Monday:** Smitten Kitchen's [Mushroom Bourguignon](http://smittenkitchen.com/blog/2009/01/mushroom-bourguignon/) **Tuesday:** pasta with broccoli and alfredo sauce (jarred), maybe some chicken **Wednesday:** leftovers **Thursday:** vegetarian shepherd's pie (from the most recent Canadian Living magazine, so the recipe isn't available online at the moment) **Friday:** c*abbage roll casserole* **Saturday:** leftovers **Su:** creamy pasta shells with peas and ham (also from Canadian Living's most recent magazine. Jamie Oliver [has a similar recipe](http://www.jamieoliver.com/recipes/pasta-recipes/mini-shell-pasta-with-a-creamy-smoked-bacon-and-pea-sauce/). I plan to sub kielbasa for ham since it's on special this week at No Frills.) **Mo:** more _[butternut squash soup](http://kneadedwords.ca/2015/10/21/soup-soup-soup/)_ (probably) **Tu:** leftovers **W:** *Lucy's marinade* chicken with salad and some kind of veg **Th:** leftovers

Some notes. The italicized meals are recipes that I have from my mother's repertoire: if they aren't posted on this blog yet, they will be at some point. "Leftovers" happens five times over fourteen days, which is actually probably too few. As it is, the buttermilk and bacon I'm using this two-week cycle was purchased on last week's budget, and the chicken I froze from the cycle before that! I tend to over plan on meals still, but it has it's uses and I still make sure to not waste food. **(1)** It gives me options when I decide I'm not up for the planned meal of the day; I can switch it with the next day's, for example. If we have a lot of leftovers still I can also decide not to cook, pushing the meal plan forward a day. If I do this, I make sure I **(2)** pay attention to how my produce is doing in the fridge. If what I was supposed to cook that day is on its last legs for whatever reason, I make myself cook it anyways, whether I want to or not. And lastly, **(3)** this often results in having ingredients leftover for the first meal or two of the two week cycle which means I don't have to get my grocery shopping done *immediately* when the paycheque lands which also lowers stress around food and budgeting.
