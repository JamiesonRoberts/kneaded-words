Cabbages seem to be in season here in Ontario. I'm guessing this because in the stores they're actually green for a chance (plus they're actually from Ontario, not imported or anything). Cabbage is pretty cheap on the regular anyways, but when produce is in season, sometimes there's great specials, such as that $0.59/pound sale for squash that was in stores a couple weeks ago.

The other thing I found on sale this week was ground beef. Extra lean "club size" packs from Superstore until Thursday I believe, if anyone nearby would like to know. They were about $15 packages (priced $4/pound); I used 1.5 lbs this week and stored two containers of just over 1lb each in the freezer for next. Excellent.

What do you make when you have pretty cabbages and cheap(er) ground beef? Cabbage roll casserole of course. Ragu is on sale at No Frills for $1 too which makes this recipe even cheaper this week. I already had a huge stash, so I didn't need to buy any.

This is not your grandmother's recipe. If you happen to have one of those, I suggest you use it instead and just cheat for time by assembling the rolls as a casserole instead of tediously rolling leaves when you want an easy night's meal. I'm sure my mother's family has one tucked away somewhere, but alas I don't have it yet. Also, if you can figure out a way to do this in one pot instead of two, do share! Two is pretty good, but it's always nice to have "one pot meals".

Cabbage Roll Casserole Warning: this recipe makes a lot of food. It also takes at least an hour to bake at the end, usually closer to an hour and a half. The prep time is pretty extensive too, from the cabbage, but I think it's worth it. The dish freezes well once cooked. You could also prepare it a day in advance of when you bake it.

- 1.5 - 2.0 lbs ground beef (lean, extra lean, whatever you prefer)
- 2-3 small onions, finely chopped
- 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup rice
- 1 cup water
- 1 jar tomato sauce
- 1/2 a large or one whole small cabbage, shredded

For the less experienced, the cabbage prep: [a youtube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MmK1N2rDINw). Note how this person turns the cabbage quarter near the end for safer slicing. They're also using a really sharp knife, so I suggest doing the first cut in half with the stem down since it's a little more stable if yours is even remotely dulled (that's me!).

Heat a glug of your oil of choice in a medium large pot on medium. Add onion and cook until softened; add garlic and cook a minute more (I also add a pinch or two of oregano). Push onions to outsides of pot and increase heat to medium high. Add beef, season with lots of ground pepper, and brown (don't be afraid to mix the onions in). Add tomato sauce, rice and water (I use a little bit extra of all three to really stretch this recipe). If you really want to save time you could skip pre-cooking the onions and garlic and add them here instead. Simmer for 10 minutes on low, being careful not to let the rice stick to the bottom of the pot. Remove from heat. In your favourite casserole dish, layer cabbage, sauce and rice, cabbage, sauce and rice. Cook in 375 F oven for 1 to 1 1/2 hours, or until cabbage is tender/easily pierced with a fork. Serve and enjoy!
