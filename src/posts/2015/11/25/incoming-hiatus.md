Hello loyal readers! ;)

Fast approaching the two-weeks-until-I'm-due-to-give-birth mark, I'll soon disappear completely from this space, but don't worry, it's only temporarily. However, by way of apology, here's a little summary of what (little) I've been cooking! More than eight months pregnant and you tend to lose a little steam...

Most of what I cooked was dictated by pantry and freezer clear out to use up those things that have just been around for almost too long:

- Whipped up a small batch of chicken soup using a partial carcass (there was one whole breast of meat, otherwise just scraps) I had frozen from a previous meal. I also utilized the leftover egg noodles from the mushroom bourguignon I made the previous week and some carrots in the veg drawer that weren't suitable for eating raw anymore.
- The other half of the ground beef pack (from the cabbage roll casserole) came out of the freezer and got cooked up for adding to tomato sauce: cooked up some onions, mushroom, seasoned with oregano and pepper. Using a 1$ jar of sauce and a 5$/1kg of cheese tortellini made for multiple cheap meals.
- Pasta happened again when I noticed my pantry jar of alfredo sauce was getting close to expired. Again, cooked up some onion and lots of steamed broccoli to give it a healthier spin (I often do zucchini with alfredo as well).
- Chicken was on special at SuperStore last week, but unfortunately I missed out by arriving the day before the sale ended and there was no product available for me. Still, I bought a club pack of chicken which got used to make Smitten Kitchen's crispy fried chicken recipe, which is my new standby when I want pan-fried chicken (you'll have to buy her book for that one), as well as Chicken "Waterzoi" (i.e. in cream sauce) from my Larousse cookbook. The leeks at the grocery store were 90% whites and light green parts which are the usable parts thus they were worth the 4$. I still have two chicken breasts leftover too.
- Still waiting for me (I hope) is a cauliflower and a couple of spicy sausages to be made into a pasta bake. (Pasta seems easy right now, it seems).
- I also think we had spinach and mushroom omelettes this week: there was definitely cheese on sale somewhere and mushrooms were a PC points item two weeks in a row. Leftovers from the week before went a long way, it seems.

Wish me luck guys! And see you on the other side of my "baby moon".
