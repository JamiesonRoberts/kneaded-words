I almost wrote this post with a sleeping baby attached to my back, but then I forgot my password and she woke up so here we are twenty minutes later and she's trying to figure out how to stand with the help of the laundry bin (not happening) while I type. In fact it's taken me several sittings over several days to finish writing this.

I seem to have been blessed with an exceptionally active, engagement craving, and sleep-resistant child so I'm only just now, eight and a half months in, finally getting back into the groove of cooking regularly.

My newest food saving trick is fried rice with leftover cooked vegetables. A little onion, some butter/oil or broth and you've got a dinner. Add in some tofu, nuts (cashews or peanuts comes to mind), or leftover meat and it would be even more filling. This is especially useful when I've cooked up a variety of things to have on hand as quick finger food for an impatient infant (who mostly just gnaws on them anyways). I'm also particularly proud of my quinoa and black bean concoction. Basically a similar premise: pan fry some onion and any other vegetable you might like (bell peppers come to mind, maybe tomatoes) with the black beans, then stir in the quinoa to heat, again adding some broth to keep it from being too dry. I also stirred in some cheddar, because yum, and ate it along side some roasted spiced cauliflower. I'm fairly certain I used some kind of recipe for the quinoa, but you'll have to forgive my absent mindedness: the baby is currently screaming for my attention while my husband tries to calm her. If you need a more descriptive recipe, I'm sure a google search will yield the same one I worked from.

I'm going to go ahead and move on from this post as its stale nature is starting to grate my nerves. Please see my next post for some more engaging writing!
