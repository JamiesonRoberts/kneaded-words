a flat

surface to my left

a flat

surface to my right

 

they

meet.

so pleasantly together in the middle, right in front.

 

white.

starch white.

horrid white.

all around…

all around…?

all around!?!

 

Can’t get out!?

Let me free?!

What’s your problem?! What’d I do? Just let me be!

 

Closing in!!

Moving towards me!!

Losing space,

losing air,

losing—!

failing—!

 

…_wait…_

 

Palms on either wall,

a little shove and I move away

(a little).

 

I can feel the space at my back…

raising the hairs on the back.

of.

my.

spine!

So,

slowly,

slowly,

I turn and find…

 

 

 

 

The **_World_**

 

in front of me;

I see it all,

 

and it sees me…

 

…

 

But,

 

Now the corner’s at my back…

 

Such a lovely presence…

 

so solid… sturdy… strong…

 

 

 

_I don’t want to leave it (!)_
