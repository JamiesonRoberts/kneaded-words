There’s a mostly empty room,

with scribbles on the door, 

and mouse crud on the floor,

because no more…

You can fill that room with lust

or with noises or with dust

but to break free and bust…?

And yet we must, someone must…!

Trees that scrape the window-pane

only scratch away in vain-

for, no one here can hear the pain

of an old forgotten bane… 

Lock the room, shut it away

no more tenants need to pay

until there be a fateful day
