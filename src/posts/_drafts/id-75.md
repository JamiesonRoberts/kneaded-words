This is a recipe to please everyone except those with a cashew or nut allergy as it's gluten free too. I've adapted it from [Canadian Living's Slow Cooker Butter Chicken](http://www.canadianliving.com/food/recipe/slow-cooker-butter-chicken-1), so if you want to make the meaty version, I suggest you start there. As a vegan option I've received suggestions to serve it with chickpeas, or tofu (pan fried first), while my brain keeps telling me to make more roasted cauliflower to serve with it. Paneer would of course me a more traditional option as well. Note: to make it vegan, I substituted vegetable broth of course as well as coconut oil for the butter, but I found it made it a little too acidic. I'm not sure if adding more coconut oil or salt would help compensate, or if I just can't find a vegetable broth I enjoy. If I could make a vegan broth with maybe some mushrooms to mellow it out I imagine that might help. I also added some cayenne powder to give it a little heat.

Chicken-free Slow Cooker Butter Chicken Sauce

1 can (796mL) diced tomatoes 2 onions, diced 3 cloves garlic, minced 2 tbsp coconut oil 2 tbsp grated fresh ginger 2 tbsp packed brown sugar 2 tsp chili powder 3/4 tsp each ground coriander and turmeric 1/2 tsp each cinnamon and ground cumin 1/2 tsp each salt and pepper 1 cup no salt vegetable broth 1/4 cup almond or cashew butter

Combine everything except the nut butter and broth in the slow cooker. Whisk the nut butter into the broth, then add to slow cooker. Cover and cook for 5 to 8 hours. Turn off heat and use an immersion blender to puree until smooth.

You might choose to cook the chickpeas or other vegan substitute in the sauce for another 30 minutes or so depending on your choice. I will be trying the chickpeas tomorrow so I'll let you know how it goes! Serve over rice with any other delicious Indian appetizers you can make or buy.
