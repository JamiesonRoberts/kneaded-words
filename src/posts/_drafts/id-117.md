Frank thoughts:

---

You know, when asking a group of mothers about the choice to have only one child, one response read something like: "Since I know she's going to be my only one, I just treasure every moment that much more!" That every first will also be the last. And I'm just thinking to myself: HOLY SHIT. That's a lot of pressure?! What if I don't enjoy it and it's gone forever? Fuck. I don't need that kind of anxiety.

---

On social media, people (especially mothers) always present a photo of their baby and say something like: "Oh, the love I have for this baby could make my heart burst! I've never been more happy!" Or something like that. Well that's bullshit. There's no way that more than 24 hours after the birth you are still ecstatic. Yes, you love that little being. But the joy of that first day is swiftly overcome by adrenaline and sheer instinct which lasts about another six weeks or so.
