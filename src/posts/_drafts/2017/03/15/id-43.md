I love cook books. And books about food. And magazines about food. I'm pretty sure it's hereditary, since my mother is the same way: we both buy and read cookbooks for fun. That and stress-bake. But everyone does that. Right?

I've gathered some of my favourite books here for you with why you should totally consider looking them up. Most I've linked to Indigo, since you can find most of them there, however don't neglect checking out used book stores such as CityLights here in London, second hand stores like Goodwill or Value Village, and even Winners which is where I found a cheap copy of Martha Stewart's "Cookies". There's always the library too! Then, if you decide it's not worth having around, you can copy out your favourite recipes and return the book, all free.
