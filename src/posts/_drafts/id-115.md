Ever have second thoughts about having kids?

You think you're so ready and it's the right point in your life and you'd love a little bundle of mush to hold and cuddle and care for.

But then you're three months in and you've spent the whole day inside hiding from the weather with a baby on your lap the whole time because they refuse to be put down and... And you start to question it all.

You message your partner to tell him how you're having regrets and he says he's been wondering the same and you go: "Fuck."

It's not like you would ever take it back or that you want the baby to disappear: you love them like crazy. But you can't help but wonder: what on earth possessed you to do this?
