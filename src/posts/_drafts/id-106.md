Alternate Title: Young

 

 

Coffee, black as night, causing ripples in the carafe.

Bubbles, light in air causing giggles from the child.

And I’m watching, just watching the world.

Enjoying the sounds, enjoying the sights.

Watching the world go by.

Clouds white like cotton, floating in shapes against blue.

Butterfly, bright as day, catching my eye walking past.

And I’m watching...

Can’t you see the world around you? Can’t you feel the breeze and hear the sounds?

Keep an eye out, ear out, face out, tongue out! 

For whatever.

And I’m watching...

Just watching, hearing, breathing, seeing, feeling...
