Our actual wedding vows (written with some help from my husband)

I vow to love you.

to share tears and laughter, breakfasts and suppers to be a best friend as well as a lover, husband,wife because it’s one part you to equal parts me to be truthful,  honest,  faithful,  respectful to have patience, to speak when words are needed and share the silence when they are not to always hold you with tenderness to never hold you back but only lift you higher to work hard for myself, but also for you to learn, to grow, to change  _together_      and remember that change can be good to have the comfort of your presence even when we are apart and to live within the warmth of your heart and call it _home._

I vow

to love you.
